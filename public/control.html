🚫 Banned movies

Banned movies can't be picked in Discord (people see them with a 🚫 so they know they spelled it right). Banning a movie that's already in the pool takes it out. Unban = you pre-approve it: the automatic rules skip it from then on.

📅 New releases

🎞️ Series

In order: a movie in a series (like Harry Potter) can only be picked once you've watched every movie before it. The later ones are on the banned list (🎞️) and come off by themselves when you watch the one before.

🔒 Locks: whoever picks the next movie of a series gets the series locked: nobody else can pick its next movie. When their movie gets watched, the next one opens only for them. If they pick other movies with their next picks (or drop it), it opens up for everyone. Change any lock by hand below.

Only one movie of a series can be in the pool at a time. Movies picked before this rule stay in.

🚫 Automatic bans

Well-known = at leastTMDB votes

TMDB doesn't say which movies have a dub, so Movie Royal guesses: cartoons, anime and well-known movies almost always have one. Small foreign movies still get banned. Unban or ban any movie by hand if the guess is wrong.

Uses TMDB's info (languages, genres, keywords), so now and then it may guess wrong: unban a movie to pre-approve it, or ban one by hand. A movie lands in the banned list the first time someone tries to pick it.

Also blocked automatically
