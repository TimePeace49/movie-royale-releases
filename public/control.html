🚫 Banned movies

Banned movies can't be picked in Discord (people see them with a 🚫 so they know they spelled it right). Banning a movie that's already in the pool takes it out. Unban = you pre-approve it: the automatic rules skip it from then on.

🎞️ Series rule

When someone picks a movie from a series (like Harry Potter or Lord of the Rings), nobody can pick another movie from that series while it's in the pool. This uses TMDB's "collection" info, so it works automatically.

🚫 Automatic bans

Uses TMDB's info (languages, genres, keywords), so now and then it may guess wrong: unban a movie to pre-approve it, or ban one by hand. A movie lands in the banned list the first time someone tries to pick it.

Also blocked automatically

✅ Movies you've watched on Letterboxd · 🏆 movies that already won · 🔒 movies someone already picked — every movie can only be picked once.
