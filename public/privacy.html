🔒 What Movie Royal sends, and where

The short version: the app runs on your PC and keeps its things on your PC. It only talks to a service when you connected that service, and only sends what that job needs.

Nothing is ever sent to the people who made this app. No statistics, no "phone home", no list of what you do. There is no server of ours that it reports to.

What stays on your PC

  • Your settings, wheels, battles, history, viewer stats and chat log: in the data folder (%APPDATA%\Movie Royal\data).
  • Your sign-ins and keys (Twitch, Discord and so on): in that same folder, nowhere else.
  • Who you raided, movie night votes, birthdays you typed in, chat bot commands and quotes.
  • ⬇️ Download backup makes a copy of that folder's data for you. It holds your sign-ins too, so keep it private.

What it never asks for

  • Your email address. The Twitch sign-in does not include it.
  • Your Twitch, Discord or Kick password. You sign in on their own page, and the app only gets a pass that you can take back there at any time.
  • Your viewers' private details. It only sees what is public in chat: names, messages, subs, bits and follows.

Who can reach the app

  • Only this PC. The app does not listen on your home network or the internet.
  • Web pages you visit can't press its buttons or read from it: anything that doesn't come from the app's own pages is turned away.
