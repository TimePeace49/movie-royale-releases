🧭 First time setup

  1. Open Settings → 🧭 Setup guide (also opens by itself the first time). It walks you through Twitch, channel points, movie search (TMDB), ratings (OMDb), Letterboxd, Discord and OBS — with a test button for each.
  2. Everything saves automatically on your computer. Nothing to remember.
  3. Use the 🧪 Test tab to try every feature before you go live.

⚔️ Running a battle

  1. Go to the ⚔️ Battle tab and pick which battle you're playing from the Playing dropdown under the game.
  2. Press ▶ START ROYALE. The game goes fullscreen, counts down 5 seconds, then the 30-second fight starts. The big timer under the button shows what's left.
  3. The last piece standing wins. A few seconds after the winner shows, fullscreen closes so you can reach the buttons.
  4. Press Next round → to put the pieces back for another round.

Change the countdown and fight length in Settings → Game.

🏷️ Putting a title on the battle screen

On the ⚔️ Battle tab, type in the Screen title box under the game (e.g. Spooky Movie Night). It shows at the top of the battle. Leave it empty to show the battle's name. Each battle remembers its own title.

⏱️ 2-minute chat timer

Press ⏱️ 2-min chat timer, then start to give chat time to type before the battle. When it runs out, the battle starts by itself. Press it again to cancel.

🏆 Claim timer & winners

  • When a Discord movie wins, press ⏱️ Start 30s claim timer. A pop-up shows chat live. If the winner types in chat, the timer stops and celebrates.
  • If the winner already typed in chat before you start the timer, they win instantly.
  • Didn't see them? Press ✅ They're here to claim it yourself.
  • The winning movie is marked watched automatically. Not watching it after all? Press ↩️ Not watching it — put it back.
  • When a wildcard (Kirb) spot wins there's no claim timer — just the winner screen.

🎬 The winner screen

For movies it shows, top to bottom: WINNER, the poster, the title, year and genre, the Rotten Tomatoes, IMDb and Letterboxd ratings, then who picked it.

Rotten Tomatoes and IMDb need a free OMDb key: get one at omdbapi.com/apikey.aspx (choose FREE), click the link in their email to turn it on, then paste it in Settings → Movie info. The Letterboxd rating works without a key. A rating shows — if a site doesn't have one.

🤖 Discord commands

The bot makes a channel called #movies-royal and pins a message listing every command. /suggest and /swap only work in that channel.

  • /suggest — pick a movie. As you type, matching movies pop up with their year.
  • /swap — trade one of your movies for a different one (1 for 1, doesn't use a pick). Only until the stream starts.
  • /mymovies (or /mine) — see your movies, picks left and luck.
  • /list — see everything in the pool.
  • /linktwitch — connect your Twitch name so chatting checks you in.
  • /help — the rules.

Already added the bot before this update? Go to Settings → Discord & picks and press Re-invite it so it can make the channel and pin the message. Rename the channel in the same place.

🚫 Why a movie can't be picked

Discord can't grey out choices, so blocked movies still show up in the list with a 🚫 and the reason — people can see they spelled it right. Picking one just explains why. A movie is blocked when:

  • ✅ you've watched it on Letterboxd (or it already won here),
  • 🚫 you banned it (🚫 Bans tab),
  • 📅 it came out this year (if that rule is on),
  • 🎞️ another movie from the same series is already in the pool,
  • 🔒 somebody already picked it — every movie can only be picked once.

🚫 Banning a movie

Open the 🚫 Bans tab, type the movie, press 🚫 Ban. You can add a reason people will see. Press Unban to undo. Banning a movie that's already in the pool removes it.

📅 New releases (this year's movies)

Every movie from the current year is blocked automatically. To let one through, search it on the 🚫 Bans tab and press ✅ Allow. Turn the whole rule off with the checkbox on that tab or in Settings → Discord & picks.

🎞️ Series rule

If someone picks a movie from a series, nobody can pick another movie from that series while it's in the pool. It uses TMDB's series info, so it just works. Once that movie is watched or removed, the series opens up again.

📏 Pick rules

  • Everyone gets 3 movies (change it in Settings → Discord & picks).
  • They can't add more until all of their movies have been watched.
  • When someone's movie wins, their other movies sit out until the new week. Weeks start Sunday.
  • Fix anyone's picks by hand on the 👥 People tab (🔓 gives a fresh set).

🔁 Swapping movies

People use /swap in Discord to trade one movie for another, 1 for 1. Swaps lock automatically when you go live on Twitch, and open again when your stream ends. You can also lock or unlock them yourself in Settings → Luck & swaps or on the Pool tab.

💬 !mymovies in Twitch chat (and Discord)

Viewers type !mymovies in Twitch chat and Movie Royale replies with their movies and picks left. !mymovies lucy shows Lucy's movies instead. In Discord it's /mymovies, or /mymovies person: Lucy. Each person can use it once every 30 seconds (looking someone up in Discord has the same cooldown). Change the command and cooldown in Settings → Twitch.

If it doesn't reply, sign in with Twitch again — replying in chat needs one extra permission.

🎟️ Kirb spots (wildcards)

3 wildcard spots join the pool every new week. If one wins, all of them leave until the next week, and there's no claim timer — just the winner screen. Add them by hand with the 🎟️ spots button on the Pool tab. Rename them or change how many in Settings → Discord & picks.

🍀 Luck

Turn it on in Settings → Luck & swaps. Every battle a Discord movie loses, it gets a bit more likely to win (1% by default — change it in Settings). It builds battle to battle and week to week. When one of your movies wins, all your other movies go back to 0.

Kirb spots don't build luck on their own — set their luck by hand with the 🍀 box on the Pool tab. You'll see each movie's luck as 🍀 +x% in the Pool.

💬 Only people who are here

Someone's Discord movies hop onto the cliff once they type in Twitch chat that stream. It resets every day. Click names on the Pool tab to add or remove people yourself. If someone typed but didn't show up, use Recent chatters to match their Twitch name.

📣 Roll call

After 3 winners in a row don't claim, Movie Royale suggests a roll call. Everyone has 2 minutes to type in chat; anyone who doesn't sits out for the night.

👥 People & your old wheel

On the 👥 People tab you can add people by hand, give them movies (＋ movie), set picks used, and link their Twitch name. To bring over your whole old wheel, open Import everyone from your old wheel and paste one person per line:

lucy_tv: Heat, Coco, Jaws (1975)

If they later use /suggest or /linktwitch in Discord, it all merges into their account.

📋 The pool & odds

  • + / − changes odds. Bigger odds = bigger piece = better chance.
  • ⏸ pauses something (off the cliff until you resume it). ✕ removes it.
  • Every entry gets its own piece on the cliff.
  • Exact odds (Settings → Game) draws the winner like a real wheel and lets the fight play it out.

➕ Making a new battle

Press + New battle and pick what goes in it:

  • 🎬 Movies — search or paste movie titles yourself. Winners get the full movie winner screen. Great for theme nights.
  • 📝 Text — paste anything, one per line. Add x3 for triple odds.
  • 🖼️ Pictures — drag in pictures.
  • 💬 Twitch chat — viewers type !join. Perfect for giveaways.

🎲 Chat battle (!join)

Open the 🎲 Chat battle tab and press ▶ Open joining. Viewers type !join in Twitch chat and pop onto the list with their Twitch picture. Press ▶ START CHAT BATTLE when you're ready. The winner screen shows the winner's Twitch name. 🧹 Clear entries empties it for next time.

📥 Importing from Wheel of Names / Marbles on Stream

On the 📋 Pool tab open 📥 Import. Paste a Wheel of Names share link, or drop a file: .wheel (Wheel of Names), .csv (Marbles on Stream or any spreadsheet), .json or .txt. Odds come along if the file has them. The + New battle box has the same thing.

Easiest Wheel of Names trick: click the entries box on the wheel, press Ctrl+A then Ctrl+C, and paste it into the list box. Always works.

🤖 Do I need a Twitch chat bot?

No. Movie Royale answers !mymovies and posts the winner from your own Twitch account while you're signed in. Turn the winner post on/off in Settings → Twitch. If it doesn't post, sign in with Twitch again (it needs the chat permission).

⎋ Leaving the battle screen

Press Esc any time to leave fullscreen. It also closes by itself a few seconds after the winner shows.

🎥 Camera & cliff size

The camera follows the action: as the cliff crumbles it zooms in so the last pieces stay big. During a battle you can scroll on it to zoom, drag to move around, and double-click to go back to the auto camera. The ➖ 🎥 Auto ➕ buttons under the game do the same (handy when the game is popped out).

Follow a piece: click any piece on the battle and the camera locks onto it (like following a marble); Tab / ▶ jumps to the next one, ◀ to the previous. Click empty ground to let go. Keys: WASD or arrows move, Q/E zoom, O shows the whole cliff, R goes back to the auto camera.

Make the cliff bigger or smaller on screen in Settings → Game → Cliff size (130% is the default). You can also turn the auto camera off there.

🎬 Instant replay

When a battle ends, the last few seconds play again in slow motion like a sports replay (with the REPLAY bug, slow-mo and a heartbeat), then the winner card shows. Click the battle, press Space, or press ⏭ Skip replay under the game to skip it. Turn it off in Settings → Game.

🔊 Volume

The ⚔️ Battle tab has a Volume box: mute, sound effects and music sliders. Changes are instant. (Same controls on the 🔊 Sounds tab.)

⭐ Turning channel points / bits on and off

Click ⭐ Points ON/OFF or 💎 Bits ON/OFF on the Battle tab, the Pool tab, the Chat battle tab or in Settings → Twitch. Green = on, red = off. Turning points off also pauses your Movie Royale rewards on Twitch so nobody spends points by accident; turning them on unpauses them.

📜 History & who won what

In 📜 History, use the search bar to find any movie or name. Switch to 👑 Who won what to see every movie each person has ever won since you started using Movie Royale.

🙈 When the winner's name shows

The winner screen only shows a name when chat picked it: Discord movies show who suggested it, and !join battles show who joined. Lists you typed in yourself just show the winner.

⭐ Channel points, bits & chaos

Viewers trigger chaos events (comets, tornadoes, earthquakes…) that hit everyone at random — nobody gets an edge.

In Settings → Twitch & channel points press ✨ Create my channel point rewards. Then change each reward's name, price, cooldown, on/off and what it does right there and press Save — it updates on Twitch instantly. Bits tiers are just below.

🔊 Custom sounds

Open the 🔊 Sounds tab. Press ⬆️ Upload next to any sound to use your own mp3/wav file, ▶ to hear it, and Reset to go back to the built-in one. Changes are live.

📗 Letterboxd

Put your username in Settings → Movie info. Movie Royale reads your whole Letterboxd profile on its own (when it starts and every 6 hours), so anything you've watched is blocked automatically — there's nothing to download. Winners count as watched right away, even before you log them.

Only if your profile is private: export your data from Letterboxd and drop the CSV files in Settings.

📺 Showing it on stream (OBS)

Press 🪟 Pop out on the Battle tab and add that window in OBS as a Window Capture (and its audio). Or go ⛶ Fullscreen and capture your screen. The Setup guide has step-by-step pictures.

🔄 Updates

When a new version is posted, Movie Royale asks “Update available — Install now?” the next time it opens. Your lists, history and settings are kept.

How to post an update (one-time setup)

  1. Make a free account at github.com.
  2. Press + → New repository. Name it movie-royale-releases, choose Public, press Create.
  3. In Movie Royale: Settings → Account & updates → type yourname/movie-royale-releases in Where updates come from.

Every time you have a new version

  1. On your GitHub repo page press Releases → Draft a new release.
  2. Choose a tag: type the version with a v, like v1.1.0 (it must be higher than the one you have).
  3. Drag the MovieRoyale-Setup-1.1.0.exe file into the box, then press Publish release.

That's it — next time Movie Royale opens, it offers the update. You can also install a setup file directly with 📦 Install update from file.

💾 Backups

Settings → Account & updates → ⬇️ Download backup saves everything to one file. ⬆️ Restore loads it back (on this or another computer).

🧪 Testing features

The 🧪 Test tab lets you: send fake chat as anyone, fake channel point redemptions and bits, check if Discord would allow a movie, run a practice battle (doesn't touch your real lists), test every connection, and play any sound.

🖥️ Keeping it running

The Discord bot and !mymovies only answer while Movie Royale is running. Closing the window keeps it in the tray by the clock. Turn on Start when Windows starts in Settings → App and you never have to think about it.

🛠️ Something's not working

  • The colored dots at the top right show each connection — hover one to see what's wrong.
  • Discord commands missing? Re-invite the bot (Settings → Discord) and wait a minute.
  • Someone typed in chat but didn't get in? Link their Twitch name on the Pool tab under Recent chatters.
  • No sound? Click inside the game once — browsers need a click before playing sound.
  • Use the 🧪 Test tab connection buttons to see a clear message for each service.
