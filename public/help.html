🧭 First time setup

  1. Open Settings → 🧭 Setup guide (also opens by itself the first time). It walks you through Twitch, channel points, movie search (TMDB), ratings (OMDb), Letterboxd, Discord and OBS — with a test button for each.
  2. Everything saves automatically on your computer. Nothing to remember.
  3. Use the 🧪 Test tab to try every feature before you go live.

⚔️ Running a battle

  1. Go to the ⚔️ Battle tab and pick which battle you're playing from the Playing dropdown under the game.
  2. Press ▶ START ROYAL. The game goes fullscreen, counts down 5 seconds, then the 30-second fight starts. The big timer under the button shows what's left.
  3. The last piece standing wins. It stays fullscreen through the replay and winner card; press Esc when you want the buttons back.
  4. Want the winner pop-up gone? Click its ✕, or press ✕ Hide the pop-up on stream on the winner card.
  5. Press Next round → to put the pieces back for another round.

Change the countdown and fight length in Settings → Game.

☠️ Sudden death

If the timer runs out and more than one piece is still standing, it's SUDDEN DEATH. Every power, including the map's own, goes off at random, faster and faster, and the cliff crumbles from the outside in until one piece is left. The timer shows how many are left. With exact odds turned on, the winner is still drawn by the odds.

🗺️ Maps

There are 16 maps. Pick one from the Map dropdown under the battle screen, or leave it on 🎲 Random for a different one each battle (it never picks the same map twice in a row).

  • ⛰️ Crumbling Cliff: the original.
  • 🪐 Deep Space: low gravity, everyone floats and slides.
  • 🌋 Lava Lake: nothing falls apart. The lava floods the land, low ground and valleys first and the hills last, so islands form. Touch lava and you're toasted. Tiles glow just before the lava reaches them. Geysers blast pieces around, and the map power Eruption floods a whole patch at once.
  • 🌈 Rainbow Road: a slippery track in space, with shells flying. The road starts falling apart somewhere different every time.
  • 🎬 Hollywood Sign: an earthquake makes the sign crumble letter by letter.
  • 🌆 Neon City: lasers sweep in from any angle, slow at first and faster later, no more than 2 at a time. Pieces jump them. Get hit and luck might dodge it, otherwise you blow up. Land a jump on another piece and you both bounce off.
  • 🥊 Final Stage: everyone starts up on three floating platforms. When a platform breaks, its pieces drop down to the big stage below. Only powers knock you off the map: you fly off screen with a KO!, unless luck brings you flying back to land on the stage.
  • 🤼 Royal Rumble: a wrestling ring. Nothing crumbles. Masked wrestlers fly off the top rope two at a time, and everyone where they land goes flying out of the ring (luck can still save them). Watch for the red circle where they'll land.
  • 🏓 Pong: a flaming ball rips straight through pieces and blows them up (luck can dodge it), faster and faster.
  • 🏜️ Dune Sea: giant sand worms burst up out of the sand and swallow pieces whole. Watch for the rings in the sand. Where a worm came up, the ground caves in and leaves a sinkhole you can fall into.
  • 🍩 Sweet Donut: a giant sugar monster takes huge bites. Whatever's in its mouth is gone, and the edge of the bite falls away as crumbs.
  • 👻 Haunted Hill: big ghosts drift through. Get close and you panic and run for the edge, until you bump into someone.
  • 🍿 Popcorn Panic: hot butter rains down. Get hit and you're buttered. The popcorn it lands on heats up and POPS at random, shooting whoever's on it off the map (luck can save them).
  • 🐉 Dragon Keep: a castle courtyard. Dragons swoop low, knocking pieces flying, and their fire burns holes in the floor.
  • 💣 Blast Zone: giant bombs drop in and trap you, then blow up in a big cross. Get out of the lines!
  • 💨 Whoopee Swamp: a goofy bean bog. Step on a whoopee cushion and PFFFT, you go flying. Gas clouds drift over and everyone runs from the smell, and pieces toot themselves around.
  • 🦸 Hero City: a rooftop fight above the city at night. A giant robot fist smashes down, a laser drone zaps, and a caped villain grabs pieces and throws them off the roof.
  • 🐀 Plague Town: a walled medieval town seen from above. Rats bite and infect you, and the sick pass it on to anyone they bump into. Infected pieces die after a few seconds unless luck saves them. Houses block everyone, and nothing crumbles.
  • ⚔️ Battlefield: all the pieces on one side, an unstoppable army on the other. Archers hit the back rows, knights crush the front, and the cavalry swings around the sides. Nothing crumbles, but you can be pushed off the edge.
  • 💨 Fart Farm: big cows stampede across the field (watch the dust at the edge), trampling pieces flat and farting on whoever's behind them. UFOs shine a spotlight, then beam pieces up into the saucer.
  • 🧱 Tumble Tower: four floors stacked up: the biggest on top, each one below it smaller. Stand on a tile for 5 seconds and it gives way, dropping you to the floor below. Fall through the bottom floor and you are out.
  • ❄️ Frozen Peak: slippery ice and falling icicles.

Each map has its own map power (Black Hole, Eruption, Blue Shell, Laser Grid…). Its button sits in the ⚡ Powers row, and you can link it to a channel point reward or bits tier like any other power.

The cliff grows with the number of entries: a few pieces get a cosy cliff, 1000 get a huge one.

🎵 Sounds for each map

In Settings → 🔊 Sounds, choose a map in Sounds for and upload sounds just for that map, like a different battle music for every map. A map's own sounds win over the all-maps ones. Reset takes the map back to the all-maps sound.

🗳️ Chat votes on the map

Press 🗳️ Chat vote under the battle screen. Four random maps show on screen, and chat types 1, 2, 3 or 4. Each viewer has one vote and can change it. When time's up (30 seconds, change it in Settings → Game) the winner is announced on screen and in chat. Press End vote now to finish early.

⚡ Using powers yourself

Every power is a button in the ⚡ Powers row under the battle screen, and they're free for you. In fullscreen, move the mouse to show the power bar on the left (it fades away again so it isn't on stream). Drag it by its title to put it anywhere; it remembers where, or press 1–9 and 0 for the first ten powers. Press one during a fight and it hits right away. Press one before the fight and it waits, then hits once the fight starts.

🔀 Shuffle

Before the battle, 🔀 Shuffle moves every piece to a random new spot on the cliff. Press it as often as you like.

📋 The list under the battle

Scroll down on the Battle tab to see everything in the current battle, with luck, odds and chance. Each row has buttons:

  • ✅ marks a movie watched.
  • ⏸ sits it out for now, and ▶ puts it back.
  • ✕ deletes it.

➕ Add at the top makes the battle match your old wheel. For Discord movies, type the person's name (or Twitch name), then start typing their movie and click it. New people are added for you, and pick limits don't apply when you add by hand. For text or chat battles, type items separated by commas.

👤 Changing who picked a movie

Press 👤 on a movie (in the list under the battle or in the 🎬 Movie Pool tab), then type a name. You can pick someone Movie Royal already knows, anyone in your Discord server, or type a new name. The movie and the pick move to that person, their /mymovies and !mymovies update, and the bot posts it in your Discord movies channel. Choose Nobody to make it your own pick.

🎨 Ball colors

Viewers type !color pink, !color #ff66aa, !color random or !color reset, and their piece on the cliff changes color. The command never replies, and each viewer can only use it once every 10 seconds, so it doesn't flood chat.

It's free by default. To make it cost channel points instead, pick Channel points under Settings → Twitch → Ball colors. Movie Royal asks before it adds anything, then adds one reward ("Royal: Ball color") where viewers type any color they want. The free command turns off while you're on points. Switch back to Free and the reward is paused, so nobody can spend points on it. Pick Off to turn colors off completely.

Why not a Twitch extension? An extension has to be hosted online and reviewed by Twitch, and viewers on phones often can't see it. A silent command does the same job without any of that.

🏷️ Putting a title on the battle screen

On the ⚔️ Battle tab, type in the Screen title box under the game (e.g. Spooky Movie Night). It shows at the top of the battle. Leave it empty to show the battle's name. Each battle remembers its own title.

⏱️ 2-minute chat timer

Press ⏱️ 2-min chat timer, then start to give chat time to type before the battle. When it runs out, the battle starts by itself. Press it again to cancel.

🏆 Claim timer & winners

  • When a Discord movie wins, press ⏱️ Start 30s claim timer. A pop-up shows chat live. If the winner types in chat, the timer stops and celebrates.
  • If the winner already typed in chat before you start the timer, they win instantly.
  • Didn't see them? Press ✅ They're here to claim it yourself.
  • The winning movie is marked watched automatically. Not watching it after all? Press ↩️ Not watching it — put it back.
  • When a wildcard (Kirb) spot wins there's no claim timer — just the winner screen.

🎬 The winner screen

For movies it shows, top to bottom: WINNER, the poster, the title, year and genre, the Rotten Tomatoes, IMDb and Letterboxd ratings, then who picked it.

Rotten Tomatoes and IMDb need a free OMDb key: get one at omdbapi.com/apikey.aspx (choose FREE), click the link in their email to turn it on, then paste it in Settings → Movie info. The Letterboxd rating works without a key. A rating shows — if a site doesn't have one.

🤖 Discord commands

The bot makes a channel called #movies-royal and pins a message listing every command (it also lists tonight's movies: #1, #2… as they get watched). When a winner claims their movie, Movie Royal also pins it in Twitch chat: “Movie #2 of tonight's stream: Heat (1995) — Lucy's movie”, followed by the movies watched earlier tonight and whose they were. 🎟️ Wild spots: when a wild (Kirb) spot wins, nothing is pinned yet. The next battle you run (any battle that isn't a chat battle or giveaway) is that wild spot battle: its winner gets the movie number, shows “MOVIE #2 TONIGHT” on stream, and is pinned as the wild spot's pick. If that winner is a text entry (not a movie), nothing is pinned: you pin it yourself. Pass on it and the next battle picks again. Try it in 🧪 Test → 📌 Movie winner pin. /suggest and /swap only work in that channel.

  • /suggest — pick a movie. As you type, matching movies pop up with their year.
  • /swap — trade one of your movies for a different one (1 for 1, doesn't use a pick). Only until the stream starts.
  • /mymovies (or /mine) — see your movies, picks left and luck.
  • /list — see everything in the pool.
  • /linktwitch — connect your Twitch name so chatting checks you in.
  • /help — the rules.

Already added the bot before this update? Go to Settings → Discord & picks and press Re-invite it so it can make the channel and pin the message. Rename the channel in the same place.

🚫 Why a movie can't be picked

Discord can't grey out choices, so blocked movies still show up in the list with a 🚫 and the reason — people can see they spelled it right. Picking one just explains why. A movie is blocked when:

  • ✅ you've watched it on Letterboxd (or it already won here),
  • 🚫 you banned it (🚫 Bans tab),
  • 📅 it came out this year (if that rule is on),
  • 🎞️ another movie from the same series is already in the pool,
  • 🔒 somebody already picked it — every movie can only be picked once.

☁️ 24/7 mode (Discord works when your PC is off)

Normally the Discord bot only answers while Movie Royal is running. Turn on 24/7 mode in Settings → Discord & picks and a tiny copy of the bot runs free on Cloudflare. /suggest, /swap, /mymovies, /list and /linktwitch work any time, with all your rules (watched, bans, series, picks…).

  1. Free Cloudflare account at dash.cloudflare.com/sign-up (confirm your email).
  2. My Profile → API Tokens → Create Token → Edit Cloudflare Workers template → your account + All zones → Create → Copy.
  3. Paste it in Settings and press Turn on 24/7 mode.

Picks made while Movie Royal is closed come in the next time you open it (and every minute while it's open). If two people grab the same movie at once, the second one gets a DM explaining why it didn't go in. The game, Twitch chat and channel points still need Movie Royal open — that's where your stream happens anyway. While the app is closed the bot shows as offline in the member list, but its commands still work.

🛡️ Backup bot server

Movie Royal Backup Bots is an optional small app. It has its own installer: download MovieRoyalBackupBots-Setup-….exe from the same release page as Movie Royal and run it on the PC that should run the backup. Then it's in the Start menu and on your desktop. Uninstall it like any program (Windows Settings → Apps); Movie Royal isn't touched. It runs Discord bots as a backup: yours, and any friend's who sends you their bot token. It starts with Windows, hidden in the tray by the clock.

Every minute it checks each streamer's 24/7 cloud bot. If the cloud bot has errors (3 failed checks in a row, or it posts a problem in #movie-royal-debug), the backup takes over: it answers /suggest, /swap, /mymovies, /list and /linktwitch with the same rules. It keeps doing that until the streamer presses ☁️ Switch back to the cloud in Movie Royal (Settings → Discord & picks, or the banner at the bottom). If a streamer doesn't use the cloud bot, it answers whenever their Movie Royal is closed. While their Movie Royal is open, Movie Royal answers itself.

💬 Chat for the viewer profiles: the backup also reads each streamer's Twitch chat, live or offline, while their Movie Royal is closed (no Twitch sign-in needed). That chat (and subs and watch streaks) goes into the viewer profiles and chat logs the next time Movie Royal opens, with the real times. Movie Royal skips anything it already counted. While you're live it also keeps track of who's in the viewer list, for watch streaks. Watch time still only counts while Movie Royal is open (that needs your Twitch sign-in).

🔥 Watch streaks: someone watched a stream if they chatted or showed up in the viewer list at some point while you were live. Their streak is how many of your streams in a row they watched. Missing one starts it over. Every stream is kept on its own. Their profile shows the streak and their last 10 streams (✅ watched, ❌ missed). Each streamer's stats stay in their own Movie Royal.

🔗 Only linked viewers: viewer stats (chats, watch streaks, watch time) are only kept for people who linked their Twitch to Discord with /linktwitch. Their profile shows the day they linked. Chat is still saved for everyone. Turn it off in Settings → Twitch to count everyone.

Movie Royal and the backup talk through a hidden #movie-royal-sync channel that only the bot can see, so no router setup is needed. Picks the backup takes come into Movie Royal the next time it opens.

Adding your own bot: one click, no copy and paste. Either press 🍿 Add my bot from Movie Royal (this PC) in the backup app, or 🛡️ Add my bot to the backup on this PC in Movie Royal (Settings → Discord & picks). A friend's bot: they press 📋 Copy my bot token in their Movie Royal (if copying doesn't work, it shows the token in a box to copy by hand), then you paste it in the backup app.

🔄 Updates: the backup app looks for its own updates when it opens and every 6 hours. The bar at the top of its window shows your version, 🔍 Look for updates, and ⬇️ Update when one is out: it downloads, closes, installs and opens again by itself, keeping your bots. Tick Also test builds to get test builds too.

A bot token gives full control of that bot, so only share it with someone you trust. To test: press ▶ Run this bot now in the backup app, then try /mymovies in Discord with Movie Royal closed, then press ⏹ Hand it back (or switch back in Movie Royal).

▶ Start / ⏹ Stop the backup server: the big button at the top of the backup app turns the whole backup on or off (every bot on the list). Stopped, it does nothing at all until you press Start, even after a restart of the PC. ▶ Run this bot now on a bot makes the backup answer that bot's Discord commands right away, without waiting for the cloud bot to have problems (while that streamer's Movie Royal is closed). ⏹ Hand it back gives the commands back to the 24/7 cloud bot (or to Movie Royal if they don't use one).

🎁 Giveaways

Open the 🎁 Giveaway tab, set the prize, then press Open entries. Viewers type !enter (you can change it). Press START GIVEAWAY and a battle picks the winner.

  • Who can enter: everyone, or only followers, subs, VIPs and/or mods. You can also require following for some days, or subbing for some months.
  • Extra odds: set extra odds for each sub tier, a loyalty bonus per sub month, and extra odds for followers, VIPs and mods.
  • Rules: stop past winners winning again (this stream, a week, a month, or ever), cap the number of entries, close entries by themselves after a time, keep bots and yourself out, and take the winner out after they win.
  • Didn't show up? Press 🔁 Reroll: their entry is taken out and the battle runs again.

Checking followers needs one more Twitch permission. If the tab says so, sign in with Twitch again once.

💬 The Chat tab

The 💬 Chat tab shows your Twitch chat with badges and emotes (Twitch, 7TV, BTTV and FFZ). On the right are 🔔 Alerts (subs, gift subs, bits, raids, follows, channel point redemptions, watch streaks and donations) and 🔎 Find someone: type part of a name to find them (click to copy it), or words they said to search old chat. Point at a message for 👤 profile, 🗑 delete, ⏱ timeout and 🔨 ban, and send messages from the box at the bottom. ⧉ Pop out on Chat, Alerts or Find someone opens just that part in its own window, and ⧉ All 3 opens all of them together. Drag them to other screens.

The first time, sign in with Twitch again (Settings → Account) so Movie Royal can delete messages, time out, ban and track watch time.

👤 Viewer profiles

Double-click anyone's name anywhere in Movie Royal to see their profile: Twitch picture and link, when their account was made, when they followed, how long they've been subbed, their watch streak and watch time, how much they chat (this stream, month, year, all time), their Discord name and movies, battle stats and wins, their respins (had at the start of the stream, used this stream, have now, and when they used them, with + / − to give or take some), and their chat log. You can time them out or ban them right there.

🔄 Updates, test builds and patch notes

Movie Royal offers new latest releases when it opens. Want to try new stuff early? Turn on 🧪 Also install test builds in Settings → Account & updates. Need an older version? 🕰️ Run an older version (same place) lists every version still on GitHub. Settings → 📝 Patch notes shows what changed in each latest release. If an update adds something new to set up (like an account), Movie Royal asks once after updating.

💡 The ideas doc

The pinned message in your Discord movies channel links to your ideas doc, where anyone can leave a comment with ideas. Change or remove the link in Settings → Discord & picks → 💡 Ideas doc.

🥊 Best of 3, 5, 7 or 9

Custom battles (the Rounds box above the list on the Battle tab), chat battles and giveaways can be a series. Whoever wins enough rounds first takes it, for example 2 wins in a best of 3. The battle screen shows the round and the score, and you start each next round yourself. To have it start by itself a few seconds after each winner, turn that on in Settings → Game (it's off at first).

💬 All chat commands

Viewers type !moviesroyal in Twitch chat, and Movie Royal replies with every command that's turned on (!mymovies, !call, !color, !join, !enter, and the wheel's !respin and !myspins…). It can only be used once every 30 seconds. You can rename it in Settings → Game.

🎬 Opening and closing movie picks

Press 🎬 Movie picks OPEN on the Battle tab to close picks, for example during the stream. While picks are closed, /suggest in Discord (and the 24/7 bot) tells people picks are closed. Their movies stay in, /mymovies and /list still work, and you can still add movies yourself. Press it again to open picks.

By default, picks lock by themselves only while a Discord movie battle is going: from when you press Start until it has its winner. In Settings → Luck & swaps you can make them lock the whole time you're live, or never, and turn off the bot's "picks are open/closed" posts.

🔮 Chat calls the winner

Before the fight starts, viewers type !call and a movie, like !call Heat (part of the title is enough, and !call kirb works for the wildcard spots). They can change their call until the fight starts. The command never replies, so it doesn't spam chat.

If their movie wins, everyone who called it is named in chat after the replay, and their own movies get +1% luck. For the luck part, their Twitch name has to be linked to their Discord with /linktwitch. You can see the calls come in on the Battle tab, and change the command or the amount in Settings → Discord & picks.

📦 Backups

Settings → Account & updates → Download backup saves one file with everything (lists, odds, luck, history, people, settings). Restore from backup loads it on any computer. Once a month Movie Royal reminds you to download one, so a copy lives somewhere other than this PC.

Movie Royal also makes its own dated safety copies in your data folder's backups folder before anything that wipes something (resetting luck, bringing back old luck, restoring a backup). It keeps the newest 30.

🚫 Banning a movie

Open Settings → 🚫 Banned Movies, type the movie, press 🚫 Ban. You can add a reason people will see. Press Unban to undo. Banning a movie that's already in the pool removes it.

📅 New releases (this year's movies)

Every movie from the current year is blocked automatically. To let one through, search it on the 🚫 Bans tab and press ✅ Allow. Turn the whole rule off with the checkbox on that tab or in Settings → Discord & picks.

🎞️ Series rule

If someone picks a movie from a series, nobody can pick another movie from that series while it's in the pool. It uses TMDB's series info, so it just works. Once that movie is watched or removed, the series opens up again.

📏 Pick rules

  • Everyone gets 3 movies (change it in Settings → Discord & picks).
  • They can't add more until all of their movies have been watched.
  • When someone's movie wins, their other movies sit out until the new week. Weeks start Sunday.
  • Fix anyone's picks by hand on the 👥 People tab (🔓 gives a fresh set).

🔁 Swapping movies

People use /swap in Discord to trade one movie for another, 1 for 1. By default, swaps (and new picks) lock only while a Discord movie battle is going: people can swap right up until you press Start, and again once it has its winner. You can change that to the whole time you're live, or never (Settings → Luck & swaps → 🔒 When adding and swapping movies lock). You can also lock or unlock them yourself in Settings → Luck & swaps or on the Pool tab.

💬 !mymovies in Twitch chat (and Discord)

Viewers type !mymovies in Twitch chat and Movie Royal replies with their movies and picks left. !mymovies lucy shows Lucy's movies instead. In Discord it's /mymovies, or /mymovies person: Lucy. Each person can use it once every 30 seconds (looking someone up in Discord has the same cooldown). Change the command and cooldown in Settings → Twitch.

If it doesn't reply, sign in with Twitch again — replying in chat needs one extra permission.

🎟️ Kirb spots (wildcards)

3 wildcard spots join the pool every new week. If one wins, all of them leave until the next week, and there's no claim timer — just the winner screen. Add them by hand with the 🎟️ spots button on the Pool tab. Rename them or change how many in Settings → Discord & picks.

🎉 Fun mode (chat battles)

On the 🎲 Chat battle tab, Fun mode makes silly battles. Power-ups: chat spends channel points on their own ball: 🛡️ Shield (saves them once), 💪 Mega ball (big and heavy) or ⚡ Turbo (faster, harder hits). They have to !join first. Press ✨ Add power-up rewards to Twitch once; the rewards are only on while a chat battle with power-ups is playing, and paused otherwise.

Wacky effects: super bouncy, low gravity, random sizes, big balls, chaos rain and disco lights. With any of it on, the fight decides the winner instead of the odds.

Chaos fits the map: on maps where the ground never breaks, Edge Collapse and Lightning don't happen, and their rewards are paused on Twitch while that map is up.

🍀 Luck

Turn it on in Settings → Luck & swaps. Every battle a Discord movie loses, it gets a bit more likely to win (1% by default — change it in Settings). It builds battle to battle and week to week. Your luck only starts over when one of your movies actually gets watched. A win counts as watched, and if you press “Not watching it — put it back”, the luck comes back too.

Cooldown: once someone's movie gets watched, they don't build luck again until the new week. LUCKY! saves are rare on purpose: only pieces that have luck can get lucky, and only once every several seconds on the whole map.

Free replacement pick: if a movie gets watched outside a battle (you mark it ✅ or it's on your Letterboxd), its person is told in Discord they can pick a new one. That one pick works any time, even with picks closed or all their picks used.

Kirb spots don't build luck on their own — set their luck by hand with the 🍀 box on the Pool tab. You'll see each movie's luck as 🍀 +x% in the Pool.

Luck also makes a piece a little bigger as it builds up (about 6% bigger for every 10% luck), and bigger pieces knock the others back harder.

Luck saves: press 💾 Save luck to keep a copy of everyone's luck. ↺ Reset luck sets it all to 0 for a few battles, and saves it first. 📂 Luck saves → Bring back puts any saved luck back (and saves what you had just before, just in case). The buttons are above the list on the Battle tab and in Settings → Luck.

Wildcard spots don't get luck on their own unless you turn that on in Settings → Luck. Then they go up at most 1% per stream, and they don't need anyone chatting.

You can see it: at 5% luck a piece starts to glow in its color, brighter every 5%. At 50% it catches fire, with flames in its color that get bigger every 5%. That part is just for looks.

Custom battles don't have luck, but you can save their odds the same way: 💾 Save odds, ↺ Everyone to 1 (saves first), then 📂 Saved odds → Bring back. The buttons are above the list on the Battle tab.

💬 Only people who are here

Someone's Discord movies hop onto the cliff once they type in Twitch chat that stream. It resets every day. Click names on the Pool tab to add or remove people yourself. If someone typed but didn't show up, use Recent chatters to match their Twitch name.

📣 Roll call

After 3 winners in a row don't claim, Movie Royal suggests a roll call. Everyone has 2 minutes to type in chat; anyone who doesn't sits out for the night.

👥 People & your old wheel

The 👥 People tab lists everyone who picked a movie or linked their Twitch in Discord (/linktwitch). Press 🔄 Refresh to pull in new Discord picks and links right away.

On the 👥 People tab you can add people by hand, give them movies (＋ movie), set picks used, and link their Twitch name. To bring over your whole old wheel, open Import everyone from your old wheel and paste one person per line:

lucy_tv: Heat, Coco, Jaws (1975)

If they later use /suggest or /linktwitch in Discord, it all merges into their account.

📋 The pool & odds

  • + / − changes odds. Bigger odds = bigger piece = better chance.
  • ⏸ pauses something (off the cliff until you resume it). ✕ removes it.
  • Every entry gets its own piece on the cliff.
  • Exact odds (Settings → Game) draws the winner like a real wheel and lets the fight play it out.

➕ Making a new battle

Press + New battle and pick what goes in it:

  • 🎬 Movies — search or paste movie titles yourself. Winners get the full movie winner screen. Great for theme nights. Need something that isn't a movie in there too? Type it and pick 📝 Add “…” as plain text at the top of the search (or 📝 Add all as plain text under Paste a big list). Plain text entries play like any other piece, without a poster.
  • 📝 Text — paste anything, one per line. Add x3 for triple odds.
  • 🖼️ Pictures — drag in pictures.
  • 💬 Twitch chat — viewers type !join. Perfect for giveaways.

🎲 Chat battle (!join)

Open the 🎲 Chat battle tab and press ▶ Open joining. Viewers type !join in Twitch chat and pop onto the list with their Twitch picture. Press ▶ START CHAT BATTLE when you're ready. The winner screen shows the winner's Twitch name. 🧹 Clear entries empties it for next time.

📥 Importing from Wheel of Names / Marbles on Stream

On the 📋 Pool tab open 📥 Import. Paste a Wheel of Names share link, or drop a file: .wheel (Wheel of Names), .csv (Marbles on Stream or any spreadsheet), .json or .txt. Odds come along if the file has them. The + New battle box has the same thing.

Easiest Wheel of Names trick: click the entries box on the wheel, press Ctrl+A then Ctrl+C, and paste it into the list box. Always works.

🤖 Do I need a Twitch chat bot?

No. Movie Royal answers !mymovies and posts the winner from your own Twitch account while you're signed in. Turn the winner post on/off in Settings → Twitch. If it doesn't post, sign in with Twitch again (it needs the chat permission).

🃏 Card battles

Your sealed card packs fight each other on stream. Each pack is a fighter with stick arms and legs, a headband and an angry face. They have a martial arts brawl in Kung Fu Town, an old Chinese town like an old kung fu movie, with health bars on top and blood as they get hurt. Every pack has the same chance (no luck) and there are no chat power-ups. Being on the 🃏 Card battles tab puts the card battle on stream.

📦 Card pool: search for a pack (the set name + "booster pack" works best), say how many you have, and press ➕ Add: its picture downloads by itself. Can't find it? ➕ Add my own pack with a picture. Each pack you own is one fighter (5 packs = 5 fighters). When a pack wins, one comes off its count: you open it. Packs are never deleted: 🙈 hides one.

The 📦 Card pool tab works like the 🎬 Movie Pool: your card battles on the left, the selected one's settings at the top (📦 every pack counts or 1️⃣ 1 of each, map, which games, ⏳ elimination pacer, ⏱️ fight length) and your packs below with − / +. Make more with + New card battle (for example a Pokémon-only one). The pack search looks on TCGplayer and also shows 🌐 pictures from the web.

Every card battle ends with a ⏪ REPLAY of the last moments. Leave the tab in the middle of a fight and it finishes quietly behind the other tab (it still counts). 🗡️ Weapons: only 2 per fight, they hit much harder, and everyone goes after whoever has one.

🤪 Wacky settings can be turned on before the fight or in the middle of it: low gravity, extreme gore (more blood, packs lose arms and legs, and their face can get chopped off), teleport switches, turbo, slow motion, giant or tiny packs, slippery street, exploding K.O.s, one-hit K.O., rage mode and weapons. In fullscreen they're in the panel on the left (keys 1–9 and 0). Try it all with 🧪 Test → 🃏 Card battle tests.

⚡🔀 Auto + Link spots

A wheel spot type: an ⚡ auto spot that always links somewhere. When it lands, the round timer pauses and Movie Royal goes straight to the wheel, battle or card battle you picked (⚙ spot settings → 🔀). When that one has its winner, it comes back and the timer waits until you spin again. Any spot can also link to a card battle now.

🖥️ Window mode & advanced graphics

Settings → Graphics: Window mode for Movie Royal itself: Window, Fullscreen window (fills the screen with no border, like a game's borderless mode) or Fullscreen. Without a title bar, use 🗕 and ✕ at the top right. Advanced graphics: render scale, how many particles, screen shake, glows, screen flashes and smooth pictures.

⏳ How a battle plays out

Knockouts are spread over the whole battle so it doesn't empty out in the first seconds (how hard: ⏳ Elimination pacer in Settings → Game; it follows the fight length, 0 = off). If it's moving too slowly, the arena throws in extra chaos (⚡ ARENA). When only 2 are left with time still on the clock, it's a 🥊 FINAL SHOWDOWN: they charge at each other, powers go off faster and the ground breaks away until one is left. When the time runs out with more left, it's SUDDEN DEATH.

📣 Chat message every hour

The 📣 Chat message button is on the Battle, Chat battle, Giveaway and Wheel tabs, and it's one switch for all of them. Turn it on and it posts in Twitch chat right away, then again every hour (change how often in Settings → Twitch → 📣 Chat messages). Each time, it posts the message for the tab you're on: Battle tab = how to add movies in Discord, Chat battle / Giveaway = how to join, Wheel = how respins work. Want it posted right now? Turn it off and on again. It stops when your stream ends.

📺 Capture window

Under ⏱️ 2-min chat timer on the Battle tab: 📺 Capture window puts a window from your PC (like your browser playing a YouTube video) small in the top-left corner of your live OBS scene, so viewers have something to watch while people join. Press it again to hide it.

First time: set up OBS in Settings → 🎥 OBS, open the video in your browser, press ⚙ next to 📺, press 🔄, pick your browser window, and save. Movie Royal makes an OBS source called "Movie Royal capture window" (with the window's sound) and puts it on top. Change its size right away with − / + or the 📏 slider under the button. ⚙ also sets the size and can hide it by itself when the battle starts. Try it in 🧪 Test → 📺 Capture window.

✍️ Spell check

Text boxes you type words in (spot names, messages, titles…) underline misspelled words in red. Right-click one for fixes or Add to dictionary. Boxes for links, passwords, keys and Twitch names are left alone.

⎋ Leaving the battle screen

Press Esc any time to leave fullscreen. It never closes by itself.

🎥 Camera & cliff size

The camera follows the action: as the cliff crumbles it zooms in so the last pieces stay big. During a battle you can scroll on it to zoom, drag to move around, and double-click to go back to the auto camera. The ➖ 🎥 Auto ➕ buttons under the game do the same (handy when the game is popped out).

Follow a piece: click any piece on the battle and the camera locks onto it (like following a marble); Tab / ▶ jumps to the next one, ◀ to the previous. Click empty ground to let go. Keys: WASD or arrows move, Q/E zoom, O shows the whole cliff, R goes back to the auto camera.

Make the cliff bigger or smaller on screen in Settings → Game → Cliff size (130% is the default). You can also turn the auto camera off there.

🎬 Instant replay

When a battle ends, the last few seconds play again in slow motion like a sports replay (with the REPLAY bug, slow-mo and a heartbeat), then the winner card shows. Click the battle, press Space, or press ⏭ Skip replay under the game to skip it. Turn it off in Settings → Game.

🔊 Volume

The ⚔️ Battle tab has a Volume box: mute, sound effects and music sliders. Changes are instant. (Same controls in Settings → 🔊 Sounds.)

⭐ Turning channel points / bits on and off

Click ⭐ Points ON/OFF or 💎 Bits ON/OFF on the Battle tab, the Pool tab, the Chat battle tab or in Settings → Twitch. Green = on, red = off. Turning points off also pauses your Movie Royal rewards on Twitch so nobody spends points by accident; turning them on unpauses them.

🗺️ NEW and UPDATED maps

In the map menus, 🔴 NEW marks maps you've never played on stream and 🔵 UPDATED marks maps that changed since you last played them on stream. The tag goes away after a battle on that map while you're live (practice rounds don't count).

🎡 The Wheel

The 🎡 Wheel tab replaces Wheel of Names. Make as many wheels as you like, give every spot a size (2 = twice the chance), a color and a rule, and spin it on stream. Press 📺 Show on stream to put the wheel on the battle screen (it also shows by itself when you spin), and ⚔️ Back to the battle to go back.

Spin, Respin, Up (move to the spot above in your list) and Down (the spot below). Type a name in "Who's spinning?" to credit someone else. Viewers can do respins and moves with channel points: in 🌐 All wheels, press Add missing rewards to Twitch. Every spin is saved in 📜 History (grouped by stream night) and 📊 Stats.

⏱️ Round timer: the timer starts when the first spin lands (2 minutes by default; set minutes or seconds in ⚙️ Wheel settings). Keep spinning: your 🔁 respins, chat's channel point respins and !respin, and moves up or down. When the timer runs out, the spot the wheel is on wins. A pop-up on the left of Movie Royal shows the timer with Stop / Start, ±30 seconds and End now (drag it wherever you like).

Spot types (⚙ on a spot): ⏱️ timer (a normal spot, the clock keeps going), ⚡ auto (the clock pauses until you spin again; shows "(auto)" on the wheel) and 🔢 countdown (each landing takes one off and the clock pauses until you spin again; shows how many are left in ( ) on the wheel). Each spot can have its own picture and its own 🔊 sound that plays when it lands there. Turn 📜 Rules off in ⚙️ Wheel settings for a plain wheel that just spins.

On ✏️ Spots, the Rule dropdown sets each spot's type. The words shown on stream when it lands are in ⚙ on the spot. To try anything on purpose, use 🧪 Test → 🎡 Wheel tests: land on a spot you pick, pretend redeems, chat, subs, bits and donations, the round timer, and a Doomsday preview that doesn't end your stream.

🔀 Linked spots: in a spot's ⚙, pick When this spot wins → another wheel or a battle. When that spot wins, the other wheel comes on stream and spins by itself (or the battle comes on stream and starts by itself, unless you untick that). Once it has its winner (and, for a movie battle, the winner has claimed it), Movie Royal comes back to the wheel you started on. With a round timer it happens when the timer runs out on that spot; with no round timer, as soon as the wheel lands there. ✌️ Double trouble and 🛑 Force stop are wheel channel point rewards too: Double trouble asks the viewer in chat which spot to double (any spot but the void), Force stop ends the round right away so the spot it's on has to be done. ⚡ Auto spots with a link don't wait: the round timer pauses and the link happens as soon as the wheel lands there; when it's done, you're back with the timer still paused. Linked spots show 🔀 → in the spot list. To try one: 🧪 Test → 🎯 Land on a spot.

⏸ / ▶ on each spot pauses it: it comes off the wheel but stays in your list, so you can put it back on any time with ▶. Spots the void takes and winners taken off the wheel are paused the same way.

📈 + / − makes a spot 10% of the whole wheel bigger or smaller (the others make room; change the % in Wheel settings).

🔁 Two respins at once: if two or more respins come in within a few seconds (you set how many), the first one spins and everyone else keeps theirs as a saved respin.

↕️ Move a spot (channel points): after redeeming, the viewer types up or down in chat and the wheel moves. It's saved in 📜 History, along with every channel point redeem.

⏪ Undo / Go back… under the wheel takes back the last thing that happened (a spin, respin, move, grow, void…), or several steps at once, if something goes wrong.

🕳️ VOID spot (Wheel settings): it respins by itself and the spot it lands on next is gone for the rest of the stream; chat sees the voided list on stream. If the void lands on itself it grows and respins again; after the number of landings you pick it has swallowed the whole wheel. Chat can only grow or shrink the void with money (subs, gift subs, bits, donations); you can always change it yourself with + / −. You can bring back 1 voided spot per month yourself; money from chat can always bring spots back.

☠️ [Your name] will return for Doomsday (optional, in the void settings): when the void swallows the wheel, a full-screen "WILL RETURN FOR DOOMSDAY!!!" shows, your mic and camera go off, and the stream ends 15 seconds later. Needs OBS connected: Settings → 🎥 OBS (or the setup guide).

Viewers use saved respins with !respin and check them with !myspins. Subs, gift subs, bits and donations pop up a small box to grow/shrink a spot, give respins or bring back a voided spot. Every wheel has its own ⚙️ Wheel settings (spin speed, sounds, looks, pop-ups, channel points).

↔️ Make a side bigger: drag the line between the spot list and the wheel (double-click it to reset). The round timer pop-up can be resized from its bottom-right corner.

📁 Folders: press 📁➕ to make a folder, then pick it on a wheel (✏️ Spots → Folder). The first menu picks the folder, the second the wheel in it.

📥 Import brings wheels over from Wheel of Names: paste a share link, or open a .wheel file. Spots, sizes, colors, pictures, the middle picture, background, title, spin time, confetti and sounds on/off all come along (Wheel of Names' own sound files can't be copied, so set your own in Wheel settings).

📜 History & who won what

In 📜 History, use the search bar to find any movie or name. Switch to 👑 Who won what to see every movie each person has ever won since you started using Movie Royal.

📋 Results shows every round's full finishing order with the time each piece went out (time into the match and the clock time), plus every power used. You can also open them from the winner card or any round. Download them as a CSV if you want.

📊 Stats shows the total battles you've run since you installed, and for every person (or every movie) their battles, wins, win %, 2nds, 3rds, 4ths, top 5s, top 10s, luck spent and how many movies they've had in. Filter by battle type (movie matches, custom battles, chat royals, giveaways) and by map, search, and click a column to sort. The wild card spot has its own record at the top.

  2. Press + → New repository. Name it movie-royal-releases, choose Public, press Create.
  3. In Movie Royal: Settings → Account & updates → type yourname/movie-royal-releases in Where updates come from.

❓ Help mode

Press ❓ at the top right of Movie Royal (or Ctrl+H), then click any button, box, checkbox or menu, on any tab or in any pop-up window. Nothing happens to it; instead a window explains what it does in plain words, with the matching part of this Help tab underneath. Press Got it and click the next thing: help mode stays on until you press Esc or ❓ again.

⌨️ Key binds

Settings → ⌨️ Key binds lists every keyboard shortcut, sorted by tab. Click a key, then press the new one (Esc keeps it, Backspace turns it off). It saves right away. ↺ Put them all back undoes your changes.

Each key only works on its own tab (Space spins the wheel only on the 🎡 Wheel tab, G starts the battle only on the ⚔️ Battle tab), and never while you're typing in a box, so typing in chat, names or titles is always safe. Only the Ctrl / Alt ones work everywhere: Ctrl+H turns help mode on and off, Alt+1 to Alt+0 jump between tabs. If you clicked inside the game screen, click outside it first.

🔇 Sound when you're in another program

By default, Movie Royal goes quiet when you click into another program (a game, OBS, your browser…) and its sound comes back when you click back into it. The stream isn't affected: the game screen in OBS plays its own sound. If you capture Movie Royal's window or your desktop sound in OBS, turn it off in Settings → App & background → 🔇 Sound.

💰 Rewards & prices

Settings → 💰 Rewards & prices has every channel point reward, bits amount and donation amount in one place, sorted by tab (Battle, Chat battle, Wheel, Raid). They're still in their usual places too: change them in either place.

  • Price: saved on Twitch right away (a reward made by hand on Twitch can only be changed on Twitch).
  • When it's on: 📏 Follow the rules (Movie Royal shows it only while it can be used), ✅ Always on, ⏸ Off (hidden on Twitch), or 1️⃣ On, then off after one use. Always on and Off beat the rules until you change it back.
  • ⏸ Pause bits amounts, or subs / bits / donations on the wheel: they do nothing in Movie Royal until you untick it.

🚀 Raid reward

A channel point reward that lets a viewer pick who you raid. Movie Royal asks them in chat, they type a Twitch name, a pop-up with a celebration sound shows it (when you're in Movie Royal; otherwise when you come back), and a 🚀 Raid → name box shows at the top of the app, left of the connection lights. Click it to start the raid. The reward hides for the rest of the stream once it's used.

Movie Royal makes the reward itself. If you already had a raid reward, it keeps its price (1500 points if there was none); one you made by hand on Twitch has to be deleted there. Turn it off, rename it or remake it in Settings → 💰 Rewards & prices. Starting raids needs one more Twitch permission: sign in with Twitch again once. Try it with 💬 Pretend chat → 🚀 Raid.

🎨 Graphics

Settings → 🎨 Graphics changes how the game screen is drawn, on stream (OBS), in the app and in pop-out windows:

  • 🖼️ Resolution: 720p and 900p are lighter for your PC, 1080p is normal, 1440p and 4K are sharper but heavier.
  • ⏱️ Frame rate: match your monitor (smoothest), or lock it at 30 / 60 FPS (or cap it at 120–240). 60 matches most OBS setups.
  • ✨ Quality: Low turns off glows and draws few particles, Medium draws fewer particles, High is everything on.
  • 📈 FPS counter: shows the frame rate, resolution and quality in the top-left corner (on stream too, so turn it off before going live). 📈 Show the FPS counter for 15 seconds shows it in the app only.
  • 🔋 Power saver (on by default): the game inside Movie Royal draws much lighter while it's hidden on other tabs or in the small preview window, so switching tabs stays smooth. What OBS shows isn't affected.

Match OBS: if OBS records 1080p at 60 FPS (OBS → Settings → Video), pick 1080p + 60 FPS lock here, and in the OBS browser source set Width 1920, Height 1080 and tick "Use custom frame rate" with 60.

❤️ Hidden health

Every ball can take only so many hard hits (bumps from bigger balls, dash hits, comets, explosions…). When it runs out, it's knocked out (K.O.!). Bigger balls are tougher. There's no health bar: a ball flashes red when it gets hurt.

✋ Claims, passes and sitting out

When the movie winner types in chat, the claim timer stops and Movie Royal asks them in chat: type !accept to watch it now, or !pass to save it for another night. It does whatever they answer (yes / no work too), and the stream shows it. A pass puts their movie back in the battle, they keep their luck and they aren't benched for the week. The ✅ Accept winner / ⏭️ Pass buttons on the winner card (Battle tab) are for doing it yourself. Accepted movies get pinned in Twitch chat (title, year, who picked it). Viewers can type !sitout to take their movies out of tonight's battles and !imback to put them back. Change the commands in Settings → Twitch.

💬 Pretend chat (🧪 Test tab): a small window where you type as any viewer. ✋ Winner types in chat opens it as the movie winner (if no movie has won, Movie Royal makes a pretend winner that changes nothing): type anything and ✅ Accept / ⏭️ Pass show up on the winner card on the Battle tab. It also has buttons for up / down after a ↕️ Move redeem, !respin, !myspins, !sitout and more, and shows Movie Royal's replies. It stays open on every tab; drag it by its top bar.
