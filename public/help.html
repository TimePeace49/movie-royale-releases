🧭 First time setup

  1. Open Settings → 🧭 Setup guide (also opens by itself the first time). It walks you through Twitch, channel points, movie search (TMDB), ratings (OMDb), Letterboxd, Discord and OBS — with a test button for each.
  2. Everything saves automatically on your computer. Nothing to remember.
  3. Use the 🧪 Test tab to try every feature before you go live.

⚔️ Running a battle

  1. Go to the ⚔️ Battle tab and pick which battle you're playing from the Playing dropdown under the game.
  2. Press ▶ START ROYAL. The game goes fullscreen, counts down 5 seconds, then the 30-second fight starts. The big timer under the button shows what's left.
  3. The last piece standing wins. It stays fullscreen through the replay and winner card; press Esc when you want the buttons back.
  4. Want the winner pop-up gone? Click its ✕, or press ✕ Hide the pop-up on stream on the winner card.
  5. Press Next round → to put the pieces back for another round.

Change the countdown and fight length in Settings → Game.

☠️ Sudden death

If the timer runs out and more than one piece is still standing, it's SUDDEN DEATH. Every power, including the map's own, goes off at random, faster and faster, and the cliff crumbles from the outside in until one piece is left. The timer shows how many are left. With exact odds turned on, the winner is still drawn by the odds.

  • 🌋 Lava Lake: nothing falls apart. The lava floods the land, low ground and valleys first and the hills last, so islands form. Touch lava and you're toasted. Tiles glow just before the lava reaches them. Geysers blast pieces around, and the map power Eruption floods a whole patch at once.
  • 🌈 Rainbow Road: a slippery track in space, with shells flying. The road starts falling apart somewhere different every time.
  • 🎬 Hollywood Sign: an earthquake makes the sign crumble letter by letter.
  • 🌆 Neon City: lasers sweep in from any angle, slow at first and faster later, no more than 2 at a time. Pieces jump them. Get hit and luck might dodge it, otherwise you blow up. Land a jump on another piece and you both bounce off.

  • 🤼 Rumble Royal: a wrestling ring. Nothing crumbles. Masked wrestlers fly off the top rope two at a time, and everyone where they land goes flying out of the ring (luck can still save them). Watch for the red circle where they'll land.
  • 🏓 Pong: a flaming ball rips straight through pieces and blows them up (luck can dodge it), faster and faster.
  • 🏜️ Dune Sea: giant sand worms burst up out of the sand and swallow pieces whole. Watch for the rings in the sand. Where a worm came up, the ground caves in and leaves a sinkhole you can fall into.
  • 🍩 Sweet Donut: a giant sugar monster takes huge bites. Whatever's in its mouth is gone, and the edge of the bite falls away as crumbs.

  • 🍿 Popcorn Panic: hot butter rains down. Get hit and you're buttered. The popcorn it lands on heats up and POPS at random, shooting whoever's on it off the map (luck can save them).
  • 🐉 Dragon Keep: a castle courtyard. Dragons swoop low, knocking pieces flying, and their fire burns holes in the floor.

The edges crumble.

Each map has its own map power (Black Hole, Eruption, Blue Shell, Laser Grid…). Its button sits in the ⚡ Powers row, and you can link it to a channel point reward or bits tier like any other power.

The cliff grows with the number of entries: a few pieces get a cosy cliff, 1000 get a huge one.

🎵 Sounds for each map

In Settings → 🔊 Sounds, choose a map in Sounds for and upload sounds just for that map, like a different battle music for every map. A map's own sounds win over the all-maps ones. Reset takes the map back to the all-maps sound.

🗳️ Chat votes on the map

Press 🗳️ Chat vote under the battle screen. Four random maps show on screen, and chat types 1, 2, 3 or 4. Each viewer has one vote and can change it. When time's up (30 seconds, change it in Settings → Game) the winner is announced on screen and in chat. Press End vote now to finish early.

⚡ Using powers yourself

Every power is a button in the ⚡ Powers row under the battle screen, and they're free for you. In fullscreen, move the mouse to show the power bar on the left (it fades away again so it isn't on stream). Drag it by its title to put it anywhere; it remembers where, or press 1–9 and 0 for the first ten powers. Press one during a fight and it hits right away. Press one before the fight and it waits, then hits once the fight starts.

🔀 Shuffle

Before the battle, 🔀 Shuffle moves every piece to a random new spot on the cliff. Press it as often as you like.

📋 The list under the battle

Scroll down on the Battle tab to see everything in the current battle, with luck, odds and chance. Each row has buttons:

  • ✅ marks a movie watched.
  • ⏸ sits it out for now, and ▶ puts it back.
  • ✕ deletes it.

➕ Add at the top makes the battle match your old wheel. For Discord movies, type the person's name (or Twitch name), then start typing their movie and click it. New people are added for you, and pick limits don't apply when you add by hand. For text or chat battles, type items separated by commas.

👤 Changing who picked a movie

Press 👤 on a movie (in the list under the battle or in the 🎬 Movie Pool tab), then type a name. You can pick someone Movie Royal already knows, anyone in your Discord server, or type a new name. The movie and the pick move to that person, their /mymovies and !mymovies update, and the bot posts it in your Discord movies channel. Choose Nobody to make it your own pick.

🎨 Ball colors

Viewers type !color pink, !color #ff66aa, !color random or !color reset, and their piece on the cliff changes color. The command never replies, and each viewer can only use it once every 10 seconds, so it doesn't flood chat.

It's free by default. To make it cost channel points instead, pick Channel points under Settings → Twitch → Ball colors. Movie Royal asks before it adds anything, then adds one reward ("Royal: Ball color") where viewers type any color they want. The free command turns off while you're on points. Switch back to Free and the reward is paused, so nobody can spend points on it. Pick Off to turn colors off completely.

Why not a Twitch extension? An extension has to be hosted online and reviewed by Twitch, and viewers on phones often can't see it. A silent command does the same job without any of that.

🏷️ Putting a title on the battle screen

On the ⚔️ Battle tab, type in the Screen title box under the game (e.g. Spooky Movie Night). It shows at the top of the battle. Leave it empty to show the battle's name. Each battle remembers its own title.

⏱️ 2-minute chat timer

Press ⏱️ 2-min chat timer, then start to give chat time to type before the battle. When it runs out, the battle starts by itself. Press it again to cancel.

🎬 Discord movie battle: the button is ⏱️ 2-min join timer + chat vote and works differently:

  • The board is reset: only people who type in Twitch chat during the 2 minutes get their movies on (plus anything you put in with ▶).
  • A numbered list of every movie shows on the right of the battle screen, with who picked it and their luck. Movies whose person hasn't chatted yet are dimmed.
  • The bot tells chat to vote who'll win by typing the number or the movie name. Votes show on the list (🗳️), and people can change their vote until the fight starts.
  • If their pick wins, they get +1% luck on their own movies next time (change the amount in Settings, where "call the winner" is).
  • When the 2 minutes are up, nothing starts by itself: press START ROYAL when you're ready. The list goes away when the fight starts.

🏆 Claim timer & winners

  • When a Discord movie wins, press ⏱️ Start 30s claim timer. A pop-up shows chat live. If the winner types in chat, the timer stops and celebrates.
  • If the winner already typed in chat before you start the timer, they win instantly.
  • Didn't see them? Press ✅ They're here to claim it yourself.
  • The winning movie is marked watched automatically. Not watching it after all? Press ↩️ Not watching it — put it back.
  • When a wildcard (Kirb) spot wins there's no claim timer — just the winner screen.

🎬 The winner screen

For movies it shows, top to bottom: WINNER, the poster, the title, year and genre, the Rotten Tomatoes, IMDb and Letterboxd ratings, then who picked it.

Rotten Tomatoes and IMDb need a free OMDb key: get one at omdbapi.com/apikey.aspx (choose FREE), click the link in their email to turn it on, then paste it in Settings → Movie info. The Letterboxd rating works without a key. A rating shows — if a site doesn't have one.

🤖 Discord commands

📁 Channel folder: every channel the bot makes goes in one channel folder called Movie Royal: the picks channel, #character-creation, and the hidden #movie-royal-debug (mods and owner only) and #movie-royal-sync (bot only), which stay hidden. #character-creation has a pinned message with how to make a character and every numbered picture (hair, outfits, makeup, facial hair, body, skin and hair colors); /character only works there, and new characters are shown off in it. Settings → Discord has the names and switches (and 🔄 Restart the Discord bot moves everything in again).

The bot makes a channel called #movies-royal and pins a message listing every command (it also lists tonight's movies: #1, #2… as they get watched). When a winner claims their movie, Movie Royal also pins it in Twitch chat: “Movie #2 of tonight's stream: Heat (1995) — Lucy's movie”, followed by the movies watched earlier tonight and whose they were. 🎟️ Wild spots: when a wild (Kirb) spot wins, nothing is pinned yet. The next battle you run (any battle that isn't a chat battle or giveaway) is that wild spot battle: its winner gets the movie number, shows “MOVIE #2 TONIGHT” on stream, and is pinned as the wild spot's pick. If that winner is a text entry (not a movie), nothing is pinned: you pin it yourself. Pass on it and the next battle picks again. Try it in 🧪 Test → 📌 Movie winner pin. /suggest and /swap only work in that channel.

  • /suggest — pick a movie. As you type, matching movies pop up with their year.
  • /swap — trade one of your movies for a different one (1 for 1, doesn't use a pick). Only until the stream starts.
  • /mymovies (or /mine) — see your movies, picks left and luck.
  • /list — see everything in the pool.
  • /linktwitch — connect your Twitch name so chatting checks you in.
  • /help — the rules.

Already added the bot before this update? Go to Settings → Discord & picks and press Re-invite it so it can make the channel and pin the message. Rename the channel in the same place.

🚫 Why a movie can't be picked

Discord can't grey out choices, so blocked movies still show up in the list with a 🚫 and the reason — people can see they spelled it right. Picking one just explains why. A movie is blocked when:

  • ✅ you've watched it on Letterboxd (or it already won here),
  • 🚫 you banned it (🚫 Bans tab),
  • 📅 it came out this year (if that rule is on),
  • 🎞️ another movie from the same series is already in the pool,
  • 🔒 somebody already picked it — every movie can only be picked once.

☁️ 24/7 mode (Discord works when your PC is off)

Normally the Discord bot only answers while Movie Royal is running. Turn on 24/7 mode in Settings → Discord & picks and a tiny copy of the bot runs free on Cloudflare. /suggest, /swap, /mymovies, /list and /linktwitch work any time, with all your rules (watched, bans, series, picks…).

  1. Free Cloudflare account at dash.cloudflare.com/sign-up (confirm your email).
  2. My Profile → API Tokens → Create Token → Edit Cloudflare Workers template → your account + All zones → Create → Copy.
  3. Paste it in Settings and press Turn on 24/7 mode.

Picks made while Movie Royal is closed come in the next time you open it (and every minute while it's open). If two people grab the same movie at once, the second one gets a DM explaining why it didn't go in. The game, Twitch chat and channel points still need Movie Royal open — that's where your stream happens anyway. While the app is closed the bot shows as offline in the member list, but its commands still work.

🛡️ Backup bot server

Movie Royal Backup Bots is an optional small app. It has its own installer: download MovieRoyalBackupBots-Setup-….exe from the same release page as Movie Royal and run it on the PC that should run the backup. Then it's in the Start menu and on your desktop. Uninstall it like any program (Windows Settings → Apps); Movie Royal isn't touched. It runs Discord bots as a backup: yours, and any friend's who sends you their bot token. It starts with Windows, hidden in the tray by the clock.

Every minute it checks each streamer's 24/7 cloud bot. If the cloud bot has errors (3 failed checks in a row, or it posts a problem in #movie-royal-debug), the backup takes over: it answers /suggest, /swap, /mymovies, /list and /linktwitch with the same rules. It keeps doing that until the streamer presses ☁️ Switch back to the cloud in Movie Royal (Settings → Discord & picks, or the banner at the bottom). If a streamer doesn't use the cloud bot, it answers whenever their Movie Royal is closed. While their Movie Royal is open, Movie Royal answers itself.

💬 Chat for the viewer profiles: the backup also reads each streamer's Twitch chat, live or offline, while their Movie Royal is closed (no Twitch sign-in needed). That chat (and subs and watch streaks) goes into the viewer profiles and chat logs the next time Movie Royal opens, with the real times. Movie Royal skips anything it already counted. While you're live it also keeps track of who's in the viewer list, for watch streaks. Watch time still only counts while Movie Royal is open (that needs your Twitch sign-in).

🔥 Watch streaks: someone watched a stream if they chatted or showed up in the viewer list at some point while you were live. Their streak is how many of your streams in a row they watched. Missing one starts it over. Every stream is kept on its own. Their profile shows the streak and their last 10 streams (✅ watched, ❌ missed). Each streamer's stats stay in their own Movie Royal.

🔗 Only linked viewers: viewer stats (chats, watch streaks, watch time) are only kept for people who linked their Twitch to Discord with /linktwitch. Their profile shows the day they linked. Chat is still saved for everyone. Turn it off in Settings → Twitch to count everyone.

Movie Royal and the backup talk through a hidden #movie-royal-sync channel that only the bot can see, so no router setup is needed. Picks the backup takes come into Movie Royal the next time it opens.

Adding your own bot: one click, no copy and paste. Either press 🍿 Add my bot from Movie Royal (this PC) in the backup app, or 🛡️ Add my bot to the backup on this PC in Movie Royal (Settings → Discord & picks). A friend's bot: they press 📋 Copy my bot token in their Movie Royal (if copying doesn't work, it shows the token in a box to copy by hand), then you paste it in the backup app.

🔄 Updates: the backup app looks for its own updates when it opens and every 6 hours. The bar at the top of its window shows your version, 🔍 Look for updates, and ⬇️ Update when one is out: it downloads, closes, installs and opens again by itself, keeping your bots. Tick Also test builds to get test builds too.

A bot token gives full control of that bot, so only share it with someone you trust. To test: press ▶ Run this bot now in the backup app, then try /mymovies in Discord with Movie Royal closed, then press ⏹ Hand it back (or switch back in Movie Royal).

▶ Start / ⏹ Stop the backup server: the big button at the top of the backup app turns the whole backup on or off (every bot on the list). Stopped, it does nothing at all until you press Start, even after a restart of the PC. ▶ Run this bot now on a bot makes the backup answer that bot's Discord commands right away, without waiting for the cloud bot to have problems (while that streamer's Movie Royal is closed). ⏹ Hand it back gives the commands back to the 24/7 cloud bot (or to Movie Royal if they don't use one).

🎁 Giveaways

Open the 🎁 Giveaway tab, set the prize, then press Open entries. Viewers type !enter (you can change it). Press START GIVEAWAY and a battle picks the winner.

  • Who can enter: everyone, or only followers, subs, VIPs and/or mods. You can also require following for some days, or subbing for some months.
  • Extra odds: set extra odds for each sub tier, a loyalty bonus per sub month, and extra odds for followers, VIPs and mods.
  • Rules: stop past winners winning again (this stream, a week, a month, or ever), cap the number of entries, close entries by themselves after a time, keep bots and yourself out, and take the winner out after they win.
  • Didn't show up? Press 🔁 Reroll: their entry is taken out and the battle runs again.

Checking followers needs one more Twitch permission. If the tab says so, sign in with Twitch again once.

💬 The Chat tab

The 💬 Chat tab shows your Twitch chat with badges and emotes (Twitch, 7TV, BTTV and FFZ). On the right are 🔔 Alerts (subs, gift subs, bits, raids, follows, channel point redemptions, watch streaks and donations) and 🔎 Find someone: type part of a name to find them (click to copy it), or words they said to search old chat. Point at a message for 👤 profile, 🗑 delete, ⏱ timeout and 🔨 ban, and send messages from the box at the bottom. ⧉ Pop out on Chat, Alerts or Find someone opens just that part in its own window, and ⧉ All 3 opens all of them together. Drag them to other screens.

The first time, sign in with Twitch again (Settings → Account) so Movie Royal can delete messages, time out, ban and track watch time.

👤 Viewer profiles

Double-click anyone's name anywhere in Movie Royal to see their profile: Twitch picture and link, when their account was made, when they followed, how long they've been subbed, their watch streak and watch time, how much they chat (this stream, month, year, all time), their Discord name and movies, battle stats and wins, their respins (had at the start of the stream, used this stream, have now, and when they used them, with + / − to give or take some), and their chat log. You can time them out or ban them right there.

💡 The ideas doc

The pinned message in your Discord movies channel links to your ideas doc, where anyone can leave a comment with ideas. Change or remove the link in Settings → Discord & picks → 💡 Ideas doc.

🥊 Best of 3, 5, 7 or 9

Custom battles (the Rounds box above the list on the Battle tab), chat battles and giveaways can be a series. Whoever wins enough rounds first takes it, for example 2 wins in a best of 3. The battle screen shows the round and the score, and you start each next round yourself. To have it start by itself a few seconds after each winner, turn that on in Settings → Game (it's off at first).

💬 All chat commands

Viewers type !moviesroyal in Twitch chat, and Movie Royal replies with every command that's turned on (!mymovies, !call, !color, !join, !enter, and the wheel's !respin and !myspins…). It can only be used once every 30 seconds. You can rename it in Settings → Game.

🎬 Opening and closing movie picks

Press 🎬 Movie picks OPEN on the Battle tab to close picks, for example during the stream. While picks are closed, /suggest in Discord (and the 24/7 bot) tells people picks are closed. Their movies stay in, /mymovies and /list still work, and you can still add movies yourself. Press it again to open picks.

By default, picks lock by themselves only while a Discord movie battle is going: from when you press Start until it has its winner. In Settings → Luck & swaps you can make them lock the whole time you're live, or never, and turn off the bot's "picks are open/closed" posts.

🔮 Chat calls the winner

Before the fight starts, viewers type !call and a movie, like !call Heat (part of the title is enough, and !call kirb works for the wildcard spots). They can change their call until the fight starts. The command never replies, so it doesn't spam chat.

If their movie wins, everyone who called it is named in chat after the replay, and their own movies get +1% luck. For the luck part, their Twitch name has to be linked to their Discord with /linktwitch. You can see the calls come in on the Battle tab, and change the command or the amount in Settings → Discord & picks.

📦 Backups

Settings → Account & updates → Download backup saves one file with everything (lists, odds, luck, history, people, settings). Restore from backup loads it on any computer. Once a month Movie Royal reminds you to download one, so a copy lives somewhere other than this PC.

Movie Royal also makes its own dated safety copies in your data folder's backups folder before anything that wipes something (resetting luck, bringing back old luck, restoring a backup). It keeps the newest 30.

🚫 Banning a movie

Open Settings → 🚫 Banned Movies, type the movie, press 🚫 Ban. You can add a reason people will see. Press Unban to undo. Banning a movie that's already in the pool removes it.

Automatic bans (each one can be turned off on that page): 🌍 movies that aren't in English at all (some other languages in the movie is fine), ⛪ really religious movies (like The Passion of the Christ or The Ten Commandments) and 🎥 pure documentaries. They go by TMDB's info (spoken languages, genres, keywords). A movie lands in the banned list the first time someone tries to pick it.

Banned movies need your OK first. Discord tells people (in the ban message and the pinned message) to ask Kirb to unban a movie they want to suggest. Change who they ask on the Banned Movies page. Unban = pre-approve: the automatic rules skip that movie from then on. Searching a movie at the top of the page shows if the rules would ban it, with ✅ Pre-approve.

🔄 Update the ban list looks up TMDB for movies that break the rules and adds the new ones (it ran once by itself the first time Movie Royal opened after the update). 🍀 Luck

🎨 Graphics

Settings → 🎨 Graphics changes how the game screen is drawn, on stream (OBS), in the app and in pop-out windows:

  • 🖼️ Resolution: 720p and 900p are lighter for your PC, 1080p is normal, 1440p and 4K are sharper but heavier.
  • ⏱️ Frame rate: match your monitor (smoothest), or lock it at 30 / 60 FPS (or cap it at 120–240). 60 matches most OBS setups.
  • ✨ Quality: Low turns off glows and draws few particles, Medium draws fewer particles, High is everything on.
  • 📈 FPS counter: shows the frame rate, resolution and quality in the top-left corner (on stream too, so turn it off before going live). 📈 Show the FPS counter for 15 seconds shows it in the app only.
  • 🔋 Power saver (on by default): the game inside Movie Royal draws much lighter while it's hidden on other tabs or in the small preview window, so switching tabs stays smooth. What OBS shows isn't affected.

Match OBS: if OBS records 1080p at 60 FPS (OBS → Settings → Video), pick 1080p + 60 FPS lock here, and in the OBS browser source set Width 1920, Height 1080 and tick "Use custom frame rate" with 60.

✋ Claims, passes and sitting out

When the movie winner types in chat, the claim timer stops and Movie Royal asks them in chat: type !accept to watch it now, or !pass to save it for another night. It does whatever they answer (yes / no work too, once they've been asked), and the stream shows it. It always asks first: even if their first message is a happy “YES!”, the movie isn't claimed until they answer the question (or you press ✅ Accept). A pass puts their movie back in the battle, they keep their luck and they aren't benched for the week. The ✅ Accept winner / ⏭️ Pass buttons on the winner card (Battle tab) are for doing it yourself. Accepted movies get pinned in Twitch chat (title, year, who picked it). Viewers can type !sitout to take their movies out of tonight's battles and !imback to put them back. Change the commands in Settings → Twitch.
