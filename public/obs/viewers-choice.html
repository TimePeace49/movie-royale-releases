🎉 Viewer's Choice for OBS

Confetti, balloons and a big pop-up on your stream when someone redeems your Viewer's Choice channel points reward. Works with just OBS open (Movie Royal doesn't have to be running). No login needed.

  1. On Twitch, your Viewer's Choice reward must have Require Viewer to Enter Text turned on (that's how it shows up in chat).
  2. Your Twitch channel name:
  3. Now redeem your Viewer's Choice reward once (on your own channel, type anything). This page catches it to learn which reward it is.
    Waiting for you to press Start…
  4. In OBS: Sources → + → Browser. Untick “Local file”, paste this into URL, and set Width 1920, Height 1080 (your canvas size):
    Put the source at the top of the list so it's over everything. Add it to every scene where you want it (right-click the source → Copy, then Paste (Reference) in your other scenes).
  5. Done. Keep this file where it is (OBS reads it from there).

Optional, add to the end of the URL: &text=YOUR+WORDS (the big text, default VIEWER'S CHOICE!) · &sound=0 (no sound) · &vol=50 (volume 0–100) · &test=1 (plays once when OBS loads it, to check).
